❓ Frequently asked questions

What is TypeBlitz?

TypeBlitz is a free typing tutor that runs entirely in your browser. Take timed or word-count typing tests, work through guided touch-typing lessons, and watch your speed climb on the stats page.

How is WPM calculated?

We use the standard formula: every 5 correctly typed characters count as one "word". WPM = (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. "Raw WPM" uses the same formula but counts every keystroke, mistakes included.

Do I need an account? Where is my data?

No account needed. Your settings, best scores, test history and lesson progress are saved in your browser's local storage only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — your keystrokes never leave this device.

What is Prank Mode? 🎭

Flip the 🎭 toggle in the header and finish a test: instead of your results you'll get a dramatic fake "analyzing keystrokes… uploading to cloud…" sequence, ending with a GOTCHA message naming your prankster. Perfect for surprising a friend — your real results are always one click away.

Can I use TypeBlitz offline?

Yes, after the first load. The only network request is the Google Fonts stylesheet; everything else (logic, sounds, word lists) is local. The sound effects are synthesized live with the Web Audio API — no audio files to download.
